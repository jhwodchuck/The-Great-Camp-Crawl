# Camp Allen at Camp Allen

## Quick Take
Camp Allen is an active traditional, faith-based overnight camp program in Navasota, TX. The official summer camp pages describe week-long residential camp sessions for ages 8-16.

## Overnight Evidence
The official summer camp page says Camp Allen offers “week long residential camp sessions for ages 8-16.” The FAQ says each camp session is one week long, with registration beginning Sunday afternoon and camper pick-up the following Saturday at 9:00 AM.

## Recent Activity Evidence
The official 2026 calendar page is live and lists multiple 2026 camp sessions from May 31 through August 1, 2026. The summer camp landing page also says 2026 camper registration opens October 1.

## Program Overview
Camp Allen is the Episcopal Diocese of Texas summer camp program in Navasota. The official site describes it as a Christ-centered camp focused on helping young people experience God, build friendships, and grow through creative, safe, and challenging opportunities. Session leadership is provided by Episcopal clergy, youth ministers, or experienced adult leaders.
