# Heart O' the Hills for Girls at Heart O' the Hills

## Quick Take
Heart O' the Hills for Girls is an active traditional overnight girls camp in Hunt, Texas, reopening for Summer 2026 at a new location under new ownership. The official site lists 2-week, 4-week, and 8-week session options for girls ages 6 through 16.

## Overnight Evidence
The official 2026 Dates & Rates page states that tuition is all-inclusive and covers meals and boarding, and the site offers 2-week split terms, 4-week full terms, and an 8-week double term. That supports classification as an overnight/residential camp.

## Recent Activity Evidence
The official homepage says Heart O' the Hills is opening for Summer 2026 in a new location under new ownership, and the 2026 Dates & Rates page publishes current session dates and fees.

## Program Overview
Heart O' the Hills describes itself as a summer camp for girls ages 6 through 16, founded in 1953. The camp emphasizes a traditional girls-camp experience centered on confidence, leadership, friendship, and personal growth. The activities page shows a broad general-camp mix including swimming, field sports, horseback riding, creative arts, performing arts, waterfront activities, and other traditional camp programming.

## Ages and Grades
