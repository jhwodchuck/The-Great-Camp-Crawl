# UNT Marching Percussion Camp at UNT Campus

## Quick Take
UNT Marching Percussion Camp is an active 2026 percussion program for high school students at the University of North Texas. The current official format includes both overnight and day-camper participation, with intensive daily instruction focused on marching percussion fundamentals, ensemble playing, and preparation for fall marching season. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

The FAQ says participants 18 and under reside in double-occupancy rooms, with camp counselors on each floor who conduct room checks and travel with minor participants to meals and camp locations. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

## Program Overview
UNT describes the camp as training high school percussionists each summer in the tradition of the UNT Drum Line. Students participate in sectionals and ensembles, receive expert guidance from percussion faculty and guest artists, and get up to 10 hours of daily instruction. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}
