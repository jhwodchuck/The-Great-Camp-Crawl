Professor Lynn Seaton’s workshop is described by UNT as an intensive week of study and performance for upright jazz bassists. The page says it is open to advanced high school students age 14+, college students, professionals, and serious amateur upright bassists, and covers upright technique, walking bass lines, small-group playing, music theory, jazz bass history, and bass ensembles. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}
