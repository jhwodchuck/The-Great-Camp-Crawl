# Academy for All (UT Austin CS) at UT Austin Campus

## Quick Take
Academy for All (UT Austin CS) is an active residential pre-college computer science program at UT Austin. The official page currently shows two 2026 variants on the same page: Academy for All and Academy for All Machine Learning.

## Overnight Evidence
The official page lists the academy type as “Residential, Overnight.” It also says students stay in student residence halls on campus and that breakfast, lunch, and dinner are included.

## Recent Activity Evidence
The official program page shows 2026 dates:
- Academy for All: June 7 - June 13, 2026
- Academy for All Machine Learning: June 14 - June 19, 2026

The same page also says applications are closed for this year and invites students to join the email list for 2027 updates.

## Program Overview
Academy for All (UT Austin CS) is a UT Austin Computer Science summer academy for high school students. The official page describes it as a week-long residential program focused on programming, teamwork, and real-world applications. The Standard edition uses Arduino microcontrollers and C++, while the Machine Learning edition uses Python and machine learning concepts.
