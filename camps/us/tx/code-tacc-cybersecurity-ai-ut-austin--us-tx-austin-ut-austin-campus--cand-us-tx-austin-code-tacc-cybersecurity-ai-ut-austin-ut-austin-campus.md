# Code@TACC (Cybersecurity / AI) (UT Austin) at UT Austin Campus

## Quick Take
Code@TACC (Cybersecurity / AI) (UT Austin) is an active one-week STEM, Computer Science, Cybersecurity, and AI pre-college residential program in Austin, TX.

## Overnight Evidence
The official page says selected students will be accepted and housed in a campus dorm at The University of Texas at Austin. The FAQ also says university housing requires card access, exits are alarmed and connected to University PD, and at least two counselors are assigned to sleep in each residential building.

## Recent Activity Evidence
The official 2026 page lists Code@TACC Cybersecurity AI for July 12 - 18, 2026. The K-12 Students overview page also lists the same camp and dates as an in-person high school program.

## Program Overview
Code@TACC Cybersecurity AI is a TACC summer program that introduces high school students to cybersecurity careers and online safety. The official page says students explore online personal safety, computer networking, mobile security, cryptography, cloud computing, cyber-crime, and ethics.
