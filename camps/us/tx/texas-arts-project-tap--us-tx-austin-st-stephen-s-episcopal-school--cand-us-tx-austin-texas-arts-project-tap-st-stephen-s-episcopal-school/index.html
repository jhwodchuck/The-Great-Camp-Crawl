    Texas Arts Project (TAP) at St. Stephen's Episcopal School¶

    Quick Take¶

    Texas Arts Project (TAP) is an active arts program in Austin, TX. The current official 2026 Senior Overnight Camp is a one-week Sunday-to-Sunday residential musical theatre intensive for ages 14-18.

    Verified Facts¶

    • Operator: Impact Arts
    • Venue: St. Stephen's Episcopal School
    • Location: Austin, TX
    • Canonical URL: https://www.impactarts.org/tap-camp
    • Program family: arts
    • Known duration signal: one-week / Sunday-to-Sunday
    • Tuition: $1,675
    • Contact email: info@impactarts.org

    Overnight Evidence¶

    The official 2026 page lists a Senior OVERNIGHT Camp for ages 14-18 and says it is a full-scale sleep-away intensive from Sunday to Sunday. It also states that tuition includes 3 meals per day, 24/7 supervision, and world-class dorms.

    Recent Activity Evidence¶

    The official page is clearly current for 2026 and lists: - Senior OVERNIGHT Camp for Ages 14-18 - June 7th-14th - registration open with payment-plan options and balances due on or before June 7th

    Program Overview¶

    Texas Arts Project is now presented under Impact Arts in partnership with St. Stephen's Episcopal School. The current TAP page describes the 2026 program as a weeklong musical theatre intensive designed to elevate students' skills to a professional level through classes, rehearsals, guest artist workshops, and a final showcase.

    Ages and Grades¶

    Grades captured from the official page: - Min grade: 9 - Max grade: 12

    The FAQ also confirms Senior Camp is for campers ages 14-18 or grades 9th-12th for the 2025-2026 school year.
