## Program Overview
Camp El Tesoro is the flagship camp property operated by Camp Fire First Texas. The venue page describes it as a multi-use camp established in 1934 on the bend of the Brazos River at Fall Creek in Granbury. During the summer, the site offers traditional overnight camp, day camp, and a grief camp program, with the standard overnight camp being the clearest fit for this record.
