# Vet Med Summer Camp (TAMU) at Texas A&M Campus

For Summer 2026, it is a 6-day residential program in College Station for rising 7th-10th graders, with a minimum age of 13 by the session start date.

## Program Overview
This current veterinary-medicine summer option introduces students to the path into veterinary school, what veterinary school is like, and career options after graduation. The official course description highlights radiology, herpetology, parasitology, large-animal hands-on experience, and a plush-pet small-animal surgery activity.
