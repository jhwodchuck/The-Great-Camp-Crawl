## Program Overview
Misty Meadows Ranch is GSSJC’s equestrian-focused overnight camp in the Treelake complex near Conroe. The official page says it offers a progressive horseback riding program, a two-level vaulting program, and additional ranch-style experiences such as gardening, goats, chickens, rifle range access, swimming, and glamping-style dorm living. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}
