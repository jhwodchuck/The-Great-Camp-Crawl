## Program Overview
This camp is Baylor’s High School All-State Choir Camp, focused on preparing students for Texas Music Educators Association All-State Choir music and auditions. The official description says students prepare All-District, Zone, All-Area, and All-State repertoire with Baylor camp faculty. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}
