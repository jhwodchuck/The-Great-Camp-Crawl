## Pricing
I did not find a clean public summer tuition table for the regular Camp Stewart sessions on the pages reviewed, so the main tuition fields remain null.

However, the official FAQ does confirm:
- initial deposit due upon application: $500
- 80% of the deposit is refundable upon written cancellation prior to March 1
- balance of camp fee is due on or before April 25 for all sessions
- tuition covers laundry, linen rental if desired, yearbook, special art supplies, and all other activity expenses
- transportation and uniforms are additional expenses
- a discount is given for two or more brothers attending in the same year
