# Anson L. Clark Scholars Program at Texas Tech University campus

## Quick Take
The Anson L. Clark Scholars Program is a highly selective, fully funded seven-week summer research program hosted by Texas Tech University in Lubbock for a cohort of 12 rising high school seniors and recent high school graduates. It stands out as a high-signal residential pre-college research opportunity because participants receive on-campus meals, on-campus room and board, weekend programming, and a $750 stipend upon successful completion of the research report.

- 2026 program dates: June 21, 2026 to August 6, 2026

- Included benefits: on-campus meals, on-campus room and board, weekend activities/programming
- Known required fee: $25 application fee

## Overnight Evidence

## Recent Activity Evidence
The official program pages list 2026 summer dates and note that the 2026 application cycle has closed, which confirms recent activity well within the current evidence window.

## Program Overview
This is a university-hosted summer research program rather than a traditional recreational camp. Texas Tech describes it as an intensive seven-week program in which scholars work hand-in-hand with faculty in a research-intensive setting. The program also includes weekly seminars, discussions, and field trips, making it a strong fit for families seeking serious pre-college academic enrichment rather than general camp programming.

## Eligibility, Ages, and Grades
Texas Tech states that applicants must be at least 17 years old by the program start date and should graduate in 2026 or 2027, meaning the program is aimed at students participating the summer before senior year or the summer after high school graduation. The university also states that applicants must be U.S. citizens or permanent residents.

## Session Length and Structure
The official 2026 dates are June 21, 2026 through August 6, 2026, which is a 47-day span and aligns with the program’s own description of a seven-week format.

Min days: 47; max days: 47.

## Pricing
Texas Tech states that there are no program participation fees. The program provides on-campus meals, on-campus room and board, and weekend activities/programming.

Expenses specifically not covered by the program are:
- $25 application fee
- Travel expenses to and from the program location
- Personal expenses
- Medical expenses

Participants who successfully complete the research project report receive a $750 stipend.
