# Corps of Cadets Leadership Camp (TAMU) at Texas A&M Campus

## Quick Take
Corps of Cadets Leadership Camp (TAMU) is an active Leadership, Pre-college overnight program in College Station, TX for academically eligible high school juniors and seniors in selected student leadership organizations.

## Verified Facts
- Operator: Texas A&M University
- Venue: Texas A&M Campus
- Location: College Station, TX
- Canonical URL: https://recruiter.tamu.edu/portal/CLI
- Program family: Leadership, Pre-college
- Minimum age: 16
- Eligibility signal: high school juniors and seniors
- Required fee: $45
- Contact email: aggiecorps@tamu.edu
- Contact phone: 979-845-9232

## Overnight Evidence
The official page describes the Corps Leadership Invitational as “an overnight program with the Texas A&M Corps of Cadets.” It says participants stay with a leader in the Corps and that the required fee includes dinner and breakfast at Duncan Dining Center.

## Recent Activity Evidence
The official page is currently live with a “February 23, 2026 Application” reference and describes the current schedule as:
- October – Juniors, and Seniors with a Texas A&M application on file
- February – Juniors, and Admitted Seniors to Texas A&M

## Program Overview
The Corps Leadership Invitational is an immersive recruiting and leadership experience connected to the Texas A&M Corps of Cadets. The official page says participants stay with a Corps leader, experience cadet life first-hand, meet the Fish Drill Team, attend a leadership panel with cadet hosts and the Hollingsworth Center for Applied Leadership Studies, and learn about scholarships, key dates, and recommendations for submitting a Texas A&M application.
