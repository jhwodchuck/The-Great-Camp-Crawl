# Summer Piano Institute (Baylor University) at Baylor Campus

## Quick Take
Baylor’s Summer Piano Institute is an active pre-college music program for rising 8th-12th graders, scheduled for June 21-27, 2026. It offers both residential and commuter participation, with residential students housed in supervised residence halls on the Baylor campus.

## Verified Facts
- Official source: https://music.baylor.edu/youth-camps-and-programs/summer-camps/summer-piano-institute
- Operator: Baylor University
- Venue anchor: Baylor Campus
- Location anchor: Waco, TX
- Grades: rising 8th-12th graders
- Dates: June 21-27, 2026
- Residential tuition: $680
- Commuter tuition: $490
- Application deadline extended to April 15, 2026
- Accepted students are notified by May 1
- Accepted students receive the registration link after acceptance

## Overnight Evidence
The official Baylor page states that students are housed in supervised residence halls and have the use of university practice and recreational facilities. That is direct support for `overnight_confirmed: true`, even though the program also offers a commuter option.

## Recent Activity Evidence
The current official page lists the 2026 session date and current application details, which supports active recent status within the record’s evidence window.

## Program Overview
The Summer Piano Institute is presented as an intensive week of study and varied musical experience within the college environment of the Baylor campus. Baylor says activities include private lessons, masterclasses, theory classes, presentations with Baylor piano faculty, elective classes in duo/duet repertoire, composition, jazz piano, music technology and recording techniques, keyboard skills, and collaborative skills.

## Ages and Grades
The official page clearly supports grades rather than ages. It states the program is open to rising 8th-12th graders. No direct age range was identified on the public page reviewed.

## Session Length and Structure
The official program dates are June 21-27, 2026, which supports a 7-day calendar-span duration. Baylor separately says residential tuition includes room and board for 6 days/nights, so the published date span and the housing-night count differ slightly.

## Pricing
The official page lists:
- Residential tuition: $680
- Commuter tuition: $490

Residential tuition includes room and board for 6 days/nights, with meals beginning Sunday dinner and ending Saturday breakfast. Commuter tuition includes lunch Monday through Friday. Camp T-shirts are also included in the published price.

Because both commuter and residential options are publicly offered, `boarding_included` is best left null on the aggregate program record unless you later split the residential track into a separate record.
