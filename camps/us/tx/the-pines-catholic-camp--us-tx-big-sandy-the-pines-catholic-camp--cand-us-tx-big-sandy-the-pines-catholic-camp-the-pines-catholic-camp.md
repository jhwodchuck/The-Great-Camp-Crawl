## Program Overview
The Pines Catholic Camp is a Catholic sleepaway camp in East Texas focused on faith formation, friendship, activities, and sacramental life. The FAQ says campers do not have to be Catholic, but the camp’s Catholic identity shapes programming through Mass, prayer, Bible study, Adoration, praise and worship, and Confession.
