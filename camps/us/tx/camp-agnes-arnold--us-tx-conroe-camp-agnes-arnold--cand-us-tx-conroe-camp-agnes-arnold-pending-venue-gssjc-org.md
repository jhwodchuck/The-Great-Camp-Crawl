# Camp Agnes Arnold at Camp Agnes Arnold

## Quick Take
Camp Agnes Arnold is an active Girl Scouts of San Jacinto overnight camp property near Conroe in the Treelake complex. For summer 2026, the official camp pages show a live overnight program built around traditional outdoor camp experiences including waterfront time at Shadow Lake, archery, hiking, crafts, swimming, and Adventure Park activities.

## Pricing
Exact Camp Agnes Arnold overnight session prices were not clearly exposed on the reviewed public HTML pages, so structured base pricing remains uncaptured.

However, the official GSSJC summer camp page does confirm:
- a $50 deposit per camper, per session is required for registration
- registration fees include camp session activities, a camp T-shirt, a summer camp patch, and a $10 trading post allowance for overnight campers
- a $50 discount applies to a camper’s second week of overnight camp
- a 10% discount applies to each additional sibling after the first registered camper

Camperships are also available for eligible Girl Scout members, with need-based awards limited to one overnight session or two day-camp sessions per girl per summer.
