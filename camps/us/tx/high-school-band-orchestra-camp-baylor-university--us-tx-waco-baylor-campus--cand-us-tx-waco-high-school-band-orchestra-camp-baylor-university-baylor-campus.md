## Overnight Evidence
The official page states that students “will be housed in regular Baylor dormitories,” with camp staff supervising students in the dorms, and says residential tuition includes “room and board for 6 days/nights.” Baylor’s summer camps FAQ also defines a residential camper as staying on campus in a Baylor dormitory with dorm room and 3 meals per day included. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

## Recent Activity Evidence
The official Baylor camp page lists the 2026 date as June 7-13, 2026. The broader Baylor Summer Camps page also lists High School Band and Orchestra Camp on the 2026 schedule and says 2026 Summer Music Camp registration opened February 18, 2026. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

## Program Overview
The official description says the annual High School Band & Orchestra Camps offer one week of intense training in string, wind, and percussion playing under Baylor faculty and staff. Students participate in large ensemble rehearsals, chamber music, string quartets, jazz ensemble, master classes, and recreational evening activities. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}
