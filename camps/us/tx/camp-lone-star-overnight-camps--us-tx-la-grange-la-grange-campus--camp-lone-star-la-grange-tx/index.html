    Camp Lone Star Overnight Camps at La Grange campus¶

    Quick Take¶

    Camp Lone Star’s La Grange campus is an active Christ-centered overnight camp program for ages 5-18 with current Summer 2026 offerings. The official La Grange summer pages show a mix of short and full-session overnight formats, with 2-night, 5-night, and 6-night experiences spanning kids and teen programs.

    Verified Facts¶

    • Operator: Camp Lone Star
    • Venue: La Grange campus
    • Location: La Grange, TX
    • Address: 2016 Camp Lone Star Rd, La Grange, TX 78945
    • Canonical URL: https://camplonestar.org/lagrange-summer/
    • Program family: faith-based, adventure, family
    • Current official age range: 5-18
    • Known overnight duration signal: 2-night, 5-night, and 6-night experiences
    • Recent activity signal: Summer 2026 pages are live
    • General contact phone: (979) 968-1657
    • General contact email: info@camplonestar.org

    Overnight Evidence¶

    The La Grange summer page explicitly says the campus offers 2-night, 5-night, and 6-night overnight camp experiences for campers ages 5-18. The overnight FAQ also references check-in and closing-program procedures for 5-night, 6-night, and weekend camp programs, which strongly confirms this as a live overnight program structure.

    Recent Activity Evidence¶

    The current official site is actively publishing Summer 2026 content. The La Grange summer landing page is live, and the individual Kids Camps and Teen Camps pages both display “summer 2026 registration is open now” language with current 2026 sessions and pricing.

    Program Overview¶

    Camp Lone Star presents the La Grange campus as a Christ-centered summer camp environment with overnight camp, day camp, and family camp offerings. For this record, the strongest fit is the overnight-camp slice of that ecosystem, which includes kids and teen overnight programs with Bible study, outdoor activities, and adventure-oriented experiences.

    Ages and Grades¶

    The official La Grange summer page says all campers ages 5-18 are invited to join the summer programs, and the Kids Camps and Teen Camps pages support that range.

    Grades remain unnormalized in this record because the current pages organize eligibility by age bands and named programs rather than a single simple grade span: - Expedition: ages 5-9 - Alpha / Alpha Max: ages 7-12 - Endeavor: ages 9-14 - Night Owl: ages 10-12 - Omega / Omega Max: ages 13-18 - Alliance: ages 13-18 - Teen Night Owl: ages 13-18 - Trek: ages 13-18 - Discovery: ages 14-18

    Session Length and Structure¶

    The previous draft only said that session length varies. The current official La Grange summer page is more specific and says the campus offers 2-night, 5-night, and 6-night overnight camp experiences.

    Examples from the current 2026 pages include: - Expedition: 2-night - Endeavor: selected 2-night sessions - Alpha / Omega / Alliance / Night Owl: 5-night sessions - Alpha Max / Omega Max / Discovery: 6-night sessions

    Structured duration capture: - min_days: 2 - max_days: 6

    This record remains flagged as not one-week-plus because the current official overnight formats top out at 6 nights.

    Pricing¶

    Public overnight pricing is available on the current Kids Camps and Teen Camps pages, though it is split across multiple program pages rather than consolidated in one pricing page.

    Published current overnight prices include: - Endeavor: $329 - Expedition: $349 - Alpha: $859 - Night Owl (ages 10-12): $859 - Trek: $899 - Omega: $929 - Alliance: $929 - Teen Night Owl: $929 - Alpha Max: $999 - Omega Max: $1,069 - Discovery: $1,129

    Structured overnight pricing range captured: - amount_min: 329 - amount_max: 1129

    Additional pricing / discount signals from the official discount page: - Bring-a-friend credits are listed as $30 for 2-night sessions and $75 for 5-night sessions - Sibling discount applies automatically, with $20 off for the second camper and each additional camper - Camperships are available, subject to limited funds

    Location and Venue Notes¶

    The official site lists the La Grange campus at 2016 Camp Lone Star Rd, La Grange, TX 78945. The broader Camp Lone Star site presents this as the main summer-camp campus, distinct from the Tomball campus, which is focused on Adventure Woods day camp.

    Contact and Enrollment¶

    Official La Grange summer page: https://camplonestar.org/lagrange-summer/

    General camp contact: - Phone: (979) 968-1657 - Email: info@camplonestar.org

    For registration-related help, Camp Lone Star’s FAQ and registration materials also reference CampWise account workflows, and some site materials reference registration@camplonestar.org for account help and registration support.

    Faith-Based / Mission Notes¶

    The faith-based classification is well supported by the official site. Camp Lone Star describes its mission as transforming lives through adventurous Christ-centered experiences, planting seeds of faith, cultivating Christian leaders, and growing the Kingdom of God. The site also publishes a formal statement of faith.

    Family and Retreat Notes¶

    The La Grange campus also supports family camp and retreat/event programming. However, those variants likely deserve separate records if you want tightly normalized session-length and pricing logic, because the family-camp structure and retreat offerings differ from the summer overnight camper programs captured here.

    Open Questions¶

    • A stable public admissions URL was not clearly exposed outside the CampWise registration flow.
    • A single consolidated public pricing page for all La Grange overnight programs was not identified.
    • Latitude and longitude still need to be captured.
    • If you want cleaner taxonomy, family-camp and retreat variants should likely be split into separate related records rather than kept folded into one overnight-camp record.
