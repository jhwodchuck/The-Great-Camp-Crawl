# TCU High School Investor Challenge at TCU Campus

## Quick Take
TCU High School Investor Challenge is an active recent college-pre-college business program at TCU in Fort Worth, Texas. The current official program is a five-day on-campus finance experience for students entering their senior year of high school, followed by a year-long virtual portfolio-management component. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

## Recent Activity Evidence

## Program Overview
TCU High School Investor Challenge is a finance-focused pre-college program hosted by the Luther King Capital Management Center for Financial Studies at the Neeley School of Business. Students receive training in securities analysis and portfolio management during the on-campus week, then manage a virtual $100,000 portfolio from summer through March of the following school year. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}
