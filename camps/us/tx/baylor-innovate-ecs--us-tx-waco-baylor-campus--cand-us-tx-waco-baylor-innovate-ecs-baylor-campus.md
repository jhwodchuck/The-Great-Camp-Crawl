## Program Overview
Baylor Innovate (ECS) is a Baylor School of Engineering & Computer Science summer camp designed to expose high school students to Baylor’s engineering and computer science programs. Campers learn from ECS faculty, complete STEM projects, tour campus and research facilities, hear from college and career speakers, and work within small groups led by current ECS students.
