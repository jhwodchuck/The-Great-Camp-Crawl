# Mo-Ranch Summer Camp at Mo-Ranch

## Overnight Evidence
The official main page calls Mo-Ranch Summer Camp a “co-ed residential Christian summer camp.” The facilities page says Loma Linda becomes the camp’s home during the summer, with air-conditioned living areas, bunk beds, and community bathrooms, and it says campers receive three hot meals plus two snacks each day. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

## Program Overview
Mo-Ranch Summer Camp is a traditional Christian overnight camp in the Texas Hill Country. The official site says campers grow in character, faith, and confidence through meaningful relationships in a Christ-centered natural setting, and the programming emphasizes the camp’s “5 Cs”: Christian Living, Creativity, Critical Thinking, Communication, and Collaboration. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}
