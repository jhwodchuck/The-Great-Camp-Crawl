# Summer Organ Camp (Baylor University) at Baylor Campus

## Quick Take
Baylor’s Summer Organ Camp is an active pre-college music program for rising 8th-12th graders, scheduled for June 21-27, 2026. It offers both residential and commuter participation, with residential students housed in supervised dormitories on the Baylor campus. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

## Program Overview
Baylor describes the Summer Organ Camp as an intensive week of study for talented young organists in a college environment. The page highlights private lessons, master classes, lecture-demonstrations, piano, harpsichord, service playing, organ-building classes, videos of famous artists, practice periods on campus organs, and student and faculty recitals. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

- Commuter tuition: $490

Residential tuition includes room and board for 6 days/nights, with meals beginning Sunday dinner and ending Saturday breakfast. Commuter tuition includes lunch Monday through Friday.
