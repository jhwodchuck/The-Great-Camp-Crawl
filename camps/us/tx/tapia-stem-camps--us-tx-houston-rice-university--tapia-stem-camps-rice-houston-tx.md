Tapia STEM Camps are active residential STEM camps at Rice University for rising 8th-12th graders. The current official program offers a 6-day / 5-night on-campus experience with dorm housing, meals, hands-on STEM projects, and communication-focused presentation work.

## Program Overview
Tapia STEM Camps are award-winning residential STEM camps that emphasize communication alongside technical learning. Campers complete STEM projects and presentations while experiencing life as a Rice student. The 2026 curriculum is centered on data centers and their impacts, including projects involving heat transfer, turbines, carbon capture and storage, college-admissions algorithms, AI-generated video detection, and hidden-message algorithms in images.
