# Honors Summer Math Camp at Texas State University

## Quick Take
Texas State's HSMC is an active six-week residential mathematics program in San Marcos with strong gifted-and-talented relevance and a current 2026 in-person session on the official Mathworks site.

## Overnight Evidence
Official materials describe HSMC as “a six-week, residential camp for high school students.” A separate HSMC daily schedule page says students live in a campus dormitory under the supervision of camp counselors.

## Recent Activity Evidence
The official HSMC page lists the 2026 session as June 21 - August 1, 2026. The official application page also states that the 2026 camp will be held at Texas State University from June 21 - August 1, 2026, and says applications are reviewed as received with acceptances beginning on or after February 15.

## Program Overview
Texas State's HSMC is a full-time, in-person intensive multi-summer program for high school students. The official page says the goal is to develop talented students through immersive and in-depth mathematical experiences and to build skills relevant to future study and careers in math, science, engineering, and related fields.
