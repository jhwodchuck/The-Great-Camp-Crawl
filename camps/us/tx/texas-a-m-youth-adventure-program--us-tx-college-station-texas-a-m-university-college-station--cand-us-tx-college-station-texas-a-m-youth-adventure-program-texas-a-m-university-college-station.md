# Texas A&M Youth Adventure Program at Texas A&M University College Station

## Quick Take
Texas A&M Youth Adventure Program is an active college-pre-college, academic-and-stem-camps, academic program umbrella in College Station, TX. The official 2026 YAP pages show multiple residential career-exploration sessions rather than one single fixed camp.

## Verified Facts
- Operator: Texas A&M University
- Venue: Texas A&M University College Station
- Location: College Station, TX
- Canonical URL: https://yap.tamu.edu/
- Program family: college-pre-college, academic-and-stem-camps, academic, stem, adventure
- Known duration signal: 5-6 day residential sessions
- Tuition range: $999-$1,299
- Contact email: yap@tamu.edu
- Contact phone: 979-845-1802

## Overnight Evidence
The official YAP site says YAP is a series of one-week courses held at Texas A&M University in College Station. It also says tuition includes housing, meals, materials, transport, instruction, t-shirt, and activities. The Aggie Camp Finder listing classifies YAP events as residential camps and sponsored residential camps.

The official course/session page also says registration for YAP 2026 is now open.

## Program Overview
Texas A&M Youth Adventure Program is a series of career-exploration courses designed for gifted and talented students. The official description says classes span a broad range of fields and are taught by Texas A&M professors and other professionals, with daily classes plus afternoon and evening recreational activities.
