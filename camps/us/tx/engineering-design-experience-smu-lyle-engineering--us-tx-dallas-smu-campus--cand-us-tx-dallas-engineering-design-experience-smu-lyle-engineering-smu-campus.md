## Program Overview
Engineering Design Experience (SMU Lyle Engineering) is an SMU Lyle School of Engineering residential summer program for rising juniors and seniors to explore engineering and STEM disciplines including civil and environmental, computer science, electrical and computer, operations research and engineering management, and mechanical engineering. Campers also spend time in the Deason Innovation Gym. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}
