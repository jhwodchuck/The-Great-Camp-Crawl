# Tapia STEM Camps (Rice University) at Rice University Campus

## Quick Take
Tapia STEM Camps are active residential STEM camps at Rice University for rising 8th-12th graders. The current official program offers a 6-day / 5-night on-campus experience with dorm housing, meals, hands-on STEM projects, and communication-focused presentation work.

## Program Overview
Tapia STEM Camps are residential STEM camps that emphasize both technical learning and communication. The current official curriculum focuses on data centers and their impacts, including heat transfer, wind turbines, carbon capture and storage, college-admissions algorithms, AI-generated video detection, and hidden-message algorithms in images.
