## Overnight Evidence
The official page states that the Overnight Camper fee includes housing, three meals per day from Wednesday lunch through Saturday lunch, and parking. The camp schedule also includes residence hall check-in on Wednesday morning and lights-out / wake-up blocks for overnight campers.

## Recent Activity Evidence
The official page lists the 2026 camp dates as June 10-13, 2026.

## Program Overview
UNT All-State Choir Camp is a UNT College of Music camp for choir students in 1A-6A Texas high schools who want to prepare repertoire for the Large School Texas All-State choral auditions.
