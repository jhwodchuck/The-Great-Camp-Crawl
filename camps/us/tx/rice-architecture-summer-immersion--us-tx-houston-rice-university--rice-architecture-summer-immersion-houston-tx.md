## Program Overview
Rice Architecture Summer Immersion is a hands-on pre-college design program for students interested in architecture school. Students work through architecture and design principles using two- and three-dimensional exercises, model making, drawing, and portfolio-oriented creative work. The program also includes interaction with Rice faculty, current students, and practicing architects.
