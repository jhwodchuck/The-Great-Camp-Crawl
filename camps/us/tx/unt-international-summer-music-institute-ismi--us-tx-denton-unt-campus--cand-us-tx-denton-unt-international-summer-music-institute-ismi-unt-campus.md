# UNT International Summer Music Institute (ISMI) at UNT Campus

## Quick Take
UNT International Summer Music Institute (ISMI) is an active Arts, Music, Pre-college program in Denton, TX with a residential overnight option for Summer 2026. The official page lists the institute dates as July 5-12, 2026. ([music.unt.edu](https://music.unt.edu/camps/ismi.html))

## Overnight Evidence
The official page lists an “Overnight camper - $1,350” fee that includes registration, tuition, housing, three meals per day from Sunday lunch through Sunday lunch, parking, and a T-shirt. It also says overnight campers arrive the morning of Sunday, July 5 for residence hall check-in and depart on Sunday, July 12. ([music.unt.edu](https://music.unt.edu/camps/ismi.html))

## Recent Activity Evidence
The official page is clearly current for 2026 and lists:

- Registration deadline: May 15, 2026
- Early bird discounts of $100 by March 1, 2026 and $50 by April 1, 2026 ([music.unt.edu](https://music.unt.edu/camps/ismi.html))

## Program Overview
ISMI is UNT’s International Summer Music Institute for string students and piano students. The official page says students participate in private lessons, chamber music, chamber orchestra, masterclasses, faculty concerts, clinics, and performance opportunities. ([music.unt.edu](https://music.unt.edu/camps/ismi.html))
