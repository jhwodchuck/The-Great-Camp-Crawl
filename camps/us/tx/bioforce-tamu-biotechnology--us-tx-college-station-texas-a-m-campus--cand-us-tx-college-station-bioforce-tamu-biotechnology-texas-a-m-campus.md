## Program Overview
BioFORCE is a biotechnology-focused STEM summer program connected to the National Center for Therapeutics Manufacturing at Texas A&M. Students participate in lectures by industry professionals, tour biotechnology research and production facilities, and complete hands-on activities such as cleanroom gowning, liquid chromatography, and bacterial transformation. The course is designed to introduce students to the biomanufacturing industry and show how medical research is translated into drugs and treatments.
