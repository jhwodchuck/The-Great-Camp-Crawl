# SeaWorld San Antonio Resident Camp at SeaWorld San Antonio

## Quick Take
SeaWorld San Antonio Resident Camp is an active overnight specialty camp program at SeaWorld San Antonio. The official resident-camp page markets 5-day resident camps for students in grades 6–12, including Expedition Camp for middle schoolers and Career / Advanced Career Camp for high school participants interested in animal care and zoological careers.

## Program Overview
SeaWorld San Antonio Resident Camp is an overnight specialty camp focused on aquatic life, marine animals, and animal-care exposure. The official page describes Expedition Camp for grades 6–8 and Career / Advanced Career Camp for grades 9–12. Career tracks emphasize animal husbandry, habitat preparation, behavior observation, and behind-the-scenes experience with SeaWorld’s zoological teams.

## Ages and Grades
The official resident-camp page says Expedition Camp and Career Camp are for participants in grades 6–12, up to 18 years of age. A clean official minimum age in years was not clearly published on the main resident-camp page, so the age minimum remains unfilled.

## Session Length and Structure
The official resident-camp page says the program runs for 5 days, beginning Sunday at 2:00 p.m. and ending Friday at 10:00 a.m. Programs are offered select weeks every summer, with an optional Saturday stayover available between back-to-back camp weeks for an additional fee.
