# Academy for Robotics at UT Austin Campus

## Quick Take
Academy for Robotics is an active residential pre-college STEM program at UT Austin. The official 2026 page describes it as a 6-day immersive overnight robotics academy for students entering grades 10-12.

## Overnight Evidence
The official page lists the academy type as “Residential, Overnight.” It also says all activities are hosted on the UT Austin campus, which is where students will stay for the duration of the program. Housing, meals, instruction, and all educational and recreational activities are included in the cost.

## Recent Activity Evidence
The official page says “Summer 2026 Enrollment Now Open!” and lists 2026 academy dates as July 26 to July 31.

## Program Overview
Academy for Robotics is a UT Austin summer academy co-hosted by the Department of Computer Science and Texas Robotics. The official page says students apply the fundamentals of mechanical design, programming, and control systems through hands-on robot building and simulation. Students explore intelligent robotics, carry out projects in simulation, and experiment with robots while learning C++ and Linux.
