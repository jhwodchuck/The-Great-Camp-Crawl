# Space Center U at Space Center Houston

## Quick Take
Space Center U is an active Space Center Houston STEM program for ages 11–18 that currently includes both multi-day day-camp offerings and a smaller set of overnight offerings under the Space U Odyssey format.

## Overnight Evidence
The official Space Center U page explicitly lists “Space U Odyssey | 5 Day Overnight Camp Ages 11-18” with 2026 sessions running June 28–July 3 and July 12–17. This is sufficient to confirm that the Space Center U umbrella includes a qualifying overnight component.

## Recent Activity Evidence
The official Space Center U page publishes multiple 2026 session dates, including May through August 2026 day-camp sessions plus 2026 overnight Odyssey sessions. The events listing also shows those 2026 Space Center U and Space U Odyssey dates.

## Program Overview
Space Center U is Space Center Houston’s immersive STEM program tied to space exploration themes. Current official offerings include mission tracks such as Lunar Expedition, Martian Expedition, AERIS, Journey to Space, Planetary Discovery, and Mission Training, with programming built around teamwork, engineering design, and NASA-inspired challenges.

## Ages and Grades
The official materials state that Space Center U serves students ages 11–18. The FAQ further splits participation into ages 11–14 and ages 15–18. A clean official grade-band mapping was not clearly published on the pages reviewed, so grade fields remain unfilled.

- Space U Odyssey overnight sessions running Sunday–Friday
