# Worship Lab (Baylor University) at Baylor Campus

## Quick Take
Worship Lab is an active Baylor pre-college worship and music program with a main 5-day July 2026 camp plus an optional 3-day songwriting pre-camp. The current public format includes both on-campus residential participation and a commuter option. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

## Overnight Evidence
The official site says the on-campus main camp includes lodging and meals, and the registration page says dorm rooms are shared between two campers. The optional songwriting pre-camp also includes lodging and meals. Because the same overall program also offers a commuter option, overnight is clearly available but not universal across every registration path. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

## Recent Activity Evidence
The Worship Lab homepage says registration is now open for the 2026 camp and lists the main camp dates as July 20-24, 2026. Baylor’s School of Music youth camps page also lists Worship Lab in its current 2026 summer lineup. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

## Program Overview
Baylor describes Worship Lab as a program designed to equip and empower students to lead in the church of today and tomorrow. The main camp is the on-campus Baylor experience, while the optional pre-camp adds a songwriting intensive before the main week. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}
