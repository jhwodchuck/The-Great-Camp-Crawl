# Shake Hands With Your Future at Texas Tech Campus

## Quick Take
Shake Hands With Your Future appears to be a legacy Texas Tech pre-college residential program rather than a currently active overnight camp. The official SHWYF page still documents a 2023 overnight-style program for grades 7–12, but Texas Tech’s current 2026 College Connect summer camp offering is Red Raider Academy, which official TTU materials describe as formerly Shake Hands With Your Future and now operates as a day camp.

## Overnight Evidence
The official SHWYF page says students are housed in Texas Tech residence halls and supervised by Texas Tech college students and staff acting as counselors. It also describes evening activities at the TTU Student Rec Center, which supports classification as a residential / overnight pre-college program in its last clearly documented form.

## Recent Activity Evidence
There is no clear official 2025 or 2026 page showing Shake Hands With Your Future itself as an active offering. Instead, Texas Tech’s current College Connect summer-camp pages show Red Raider Academy, and TTU’s event registration page identifies Red Raider Academy as formerly Shake Hands With Your Future. Because the currently active successor is documented but the SHWYF-branded residential program is not, active status for this exact record remains unresolved.

## Program Overview
In its last clearly documented official form, Shake Hands With Your Future let students experience university life, enroll in two classes, and explore fields across STEM, business, telemedicine, art, forensics, performing arts, robotics, and related academic areas. The program also included evening recreation and college-readiness exposure on the Texas Tech campus.
