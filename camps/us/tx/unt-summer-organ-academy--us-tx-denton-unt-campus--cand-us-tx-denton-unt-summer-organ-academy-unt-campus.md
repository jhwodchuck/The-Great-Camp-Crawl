## Program Overview
UNT describes the academy as an exciting week-long organ program centered on repertoire, improvisation, liturgical playing, and keyboard skills using UNT’s organ resources. The program also includes harpsichord/figured-bass exposure and a Dallas excursion to visit major instruments and venues, concluding with student recitals at UNT. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}
