# Middle School Band & Orchestra Camp (Baylor University) at Baylor Campus

## Quick Take
Middle School Band & Orchestra Camp is an active Baylor music camp for students entering grades 7-9, scheduled for June 14-20, 2026. It offers both residential and commuter participation, with residential students housed in Baylor dormitories and commuter students receiving lunch Monday through Friday.

## Verified Facts
- Operator: Baylor University
- Venue anchor: Baylor Campus
- Location anchor: Waco, TX
- Grades: 7-9
- Dates: June 14-20, 2026
- Residential tuition: $680
- Commuter tuition: $490
- Non-refundable deposit: $200
- Registration deadline: May 29, 2026
- Contact: beverly_shultz@baylor.edu | (254) 710-3571

## Overnight Evidence
The official Baylor page states that students are housed in regular Baylor dormitories and receive meals in the university dining halls. It also says residential tuition includes room and board for 6 days/nights and that camp students in the dormitories are under careful supervision by camp staff.

## Recent Activity Evidence
The current official page lists the 2026 session dates and says 2026 summer music camp registration opens on February 18, which supports active recent status.

## Program Overview
Baylor describes the camp as one week of intense, fun-filled training in performance for middle school band and orchestra students. The schedule includes large ensemble rehearsals, sectional rehearsals, group lessons, and supervised recreational activities, and the camp concludes with a Saturday morning Grand Concert.

## Ages and Grades
The official page supports grade eligibility rather than age eligibility. It states that the camp is open to students who will enter grades 7-9 in the fall and that students must have played their instrument for at least one year.

## Session Length and Structure
The official program dates are June 14-20, 2026, which supports a 7-day calendar-span duration. Baylor separately states that residential tuition covers room and board for 6 days/nights, so the calendar span and housing-night count differ slightly.

## Pricing
The official page lists:
- Residential tuition: $680
- Commuter tuition: $490

It also states that a non-refundable $200 deposit is required at registration and is applied toward the total cost. Residential tuition includes room and board for 6 days/nights, while commuter tuition includes lunch Monday through Friday. Camp T-shirts and movie tickets are included in the published price.

## Housing and Meals
The official housing section says students are housed in regular Baylor dormitories, eat meals in university dining halls, and may request roommates. Rooms accommodate two students, and Baylor notes it will not know the exact dormitory assignment until May. Meals begin with dinner on Sunday and end with breakfast on Saturday.
