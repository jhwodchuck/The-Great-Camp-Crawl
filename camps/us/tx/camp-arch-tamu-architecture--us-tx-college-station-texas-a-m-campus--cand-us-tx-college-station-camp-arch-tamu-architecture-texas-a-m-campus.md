# Camp ARCH (TAMU Architecture) at Texas A&M Campus

## Program Overview
Camp ARCH is Texas A&M College of Architecture’s summer outreach program for high-achieving high school students. Students choose one focus area from Architecture, Construction Science, Landscape Architecture, or Urban Planning and participate in faculty-led classes, hands-on design work, and campus activities.
