## Program Overview
Schieffer Summer Journalism Camp is a TCU journalism experience for students interested in digital reporting and college readiness. The camp is led by faculty, staff, and students in the TCU Department of Journalism, with support from media professionals in the Dallas–Fort Worth market. Students report and develop stories, produce content for a newscast, and write for the program website. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}
