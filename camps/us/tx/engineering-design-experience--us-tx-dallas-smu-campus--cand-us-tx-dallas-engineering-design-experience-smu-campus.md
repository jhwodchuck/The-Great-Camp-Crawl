# Engineering Design Experience at SMU Campus

## Quick Take
Engineering Design Experience is an active residential engineering pre-college program at SMU in Dallas for rising 11th and 12th grade students.

## Verified Facts
- Operator: SMU Lyle School of Engineering
- Venue: SMU Campus
- Location: Dallas, TX
- Canonical URL: https://www.smu.edu/lyle/centers-and-institutes/caruth/youth-programs-and-camps/engineering-summer-camps/engineering-design-experience
- Program family: college-pre-college, academic-stem, stem
- Known duration signal: 5-day residential camp
- Cost: $1,400
- Contact email: ciee@lyle.smu.edu
- Contact phone: 214-768-4262

## Overnight Evidence
The official page calls it a residential camp for rising 11th and 12th grade students. It says campers experience college life including dorms and independent living, that counselors and mentors stay in the dorms with campers, and that room and all meals are provided with breakfast in the SMU Dining Hall.

## Program Overview
Engineering Design Experience is an SMU Lyle School of Engineering residential summer program designed for rising juniors and seniors to explore college readiness and engineering pathways including civil and environmental, computer science, electrical and computer, operations research and engineering management, and mechanical engineering.
