Camp Cloud Rim is Girl Scouts of Utah’s mountain camp near Park City on the shore of Lake Brimhall. The official camp pages highlight lake paddling, canoeing, sailing, kayaking, swimming, pottery, rock climbing, hiking, backpacking, and outdoor skills.
